NAIB · Music Guide

NAIB helps musicians find the right course, studio, instrument, research room, or creative pathway without replacing the teacher, composer, performer, or learner. The Refrain is the formal music-course home; Bazaar Art supplies a connected creative studio world; ARSHIF supports deeper music and cultural study; Aurora offers an optional reset when a musician needs quiet.

The Refrain Conservatory.

Music electives and formal music courses belong here. The conservatory can grow from early creative experiences through secondary electives, advanced theory, composition, performance, production, research, and lifelong independent study.
