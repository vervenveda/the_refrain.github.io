Editing guide

Click a clip to select it, click inside it to place the playhead, and Split. Drag a range to trim, crop or silence it. Mend rejoins contiguous pieces from the same take. Use Move and fades to combine different recordings. Undo and redo preserve up to 100 editing decisions during this session. Export before closing; audio and undo history are not saved automatically.
