Vocal mixer

Adjust each voice independently while listening. Solo isolates selected voices; mute always takes priority.

Output ready

Center is balanced; left and right place vocals across the stereo field. WAV export uses the same channel settings.

Project & audio export

Keep the original takes with the JSON file. Reload matching originals before restoring; the project checks their SHA-256 fingerprints. Exported WAV contains the edited mix, not changes to the original files.

Editing guide

Use Move clips to drag recordings between voice lanes or along the timeline. Enable Snap to align edges, or use Close gap before clip. Switch to Select range, click inside a clip to place the playhead, and Split. Drag a range to trim, crop or silence it. Mend rejoins contiguous pieces from the same take. Use Move and fades to combine different recordings. Undo and redo preserve up to 100 editing decisions during this session. Export before closing; audio and undo history are not saved automatically. Mixer settings are included in version 2 project exports; older projects restore with default mixer settings. Mixer adjustments are independent of clip undo and redo.
